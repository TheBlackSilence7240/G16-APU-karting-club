Available Equipment

Get everything you need to participate safely and comfortably in karting.

Karts

Karts will be provided at the training location for all members that have paid the membership fee.

HELMETS

A protective full-face helmet to protect yourself during karting in case of accidents.

All members must bring their own helmet as it is not provided by us during sessions.

Safety Equipment & Important Information

The following equipment is provided to support participant safety and comfort during karting activities.

Fire Extinguishers

Fire extinguishers are essential safety equipment kept available at the karting facility for emergency situations involving fire.

They allow staff to respond quickly to a fire and help maintain a safer environment around the track and karting area.

Fire-Resistant Overalls

Fire-resistant overalls provide additional protection for drivers during karting sessions.

Important: Fire-resistant overalls are required for all karting sessions.

Protective Gloves

Protective gloves help protect the hands while driving and provide a secure grip on the steering wheel.

Protective Boots

Protective karting boots provide additional protection for the feet while driving.

The anti-slip soles help provide better stability and control during karting activities.

Waterproof Clothing

Waterproof jackets and trousers can be used for outdoor karting activities during wet weather conditions.

This equipment helps keep participants more comfortable when racing on an outdoor track in rainy conditions.

First-Aid Kit

A first-aid kit is available at the karting facility to provide immediate basic assistance for minor injuries or emergencies.

Emergency Communication Device

Emergency communication equipment is available to allow staff to communicate quickly during accidents or other track emergencies.
