Available Equipment

Get everything you need to participate safely and comfortably in karting.

Karts

Karts will be provided at the training location for all members that have paid the membership fee.

HELMETS

A protective full-face helmet to protect yourself during karting in case of accidents.

All members must bring their own helmet as it is not provided by us during sessions.

Safety Equipment & Important Information

The following equipment is provided to support participant safety and comfort during karting activities.

Fire Extinguishers

Fire Extinguishers are always present in the karting track.

They allow staff to respond quickly to a fire and help maintain a safer environment around the track and karting area.

First-Aid Kit

A first-aid kit is available at the karting facility to provide immediate basic assistance for minor injuries or emergencies.
