Safety first, speed second:

CLUB RULES

To keep every session safe and fun for everyone, all members are expected to follow these rules at all times.

Safety Rules

  • Helmets and provided safety gear must be worn at all times on track.
  • No overtaking under yellow flag conditions.
  • Follow marshal and advisor instructions immediately.
  • Report any kart or track issues right away.
  • No passengers — one driver per kart.

General Conduct

  • Respect fellow members, advisors, and staff at all times.
  • Reckless or dangerous driving will result in a session ban.
  • Arrive on time for scheduled training sessions.
  • Keep the pit and paddock area clean and tidy.
  • No alcohol or substances before or during sessions.

Membership

  • Membership is open to all registered APU students.
  • Members must complete the registration form before joining sessions.
  • Attendance at the safety briefing is required before your first session.
  • Repeated rule violations may result in suspension from the club.
