"Deus Celeritatem Ad Victoriam Det."


We are the KARTING CLUB for Asia Pacific University. The Club was founded in the year 2024 and has grown stronger ever since. The Club is made specifically for the karting activity as we found the line up of clubs during that time was lacking a bit of thrill so this club was created. As the Official Karting Club of APU, our primary objecive is to train people in the art of karting and most importantly have fun while maintaining our safety of course.

OUR MISSIONS

BUILD TEAMWORK

We want to encourage teamwork and communication among members through group activities, training sessions, and competitions.

DEVELOP CONFIDENCE

We want to provide members with opportunities to build confidence, discipline, and decision-making skills through karting.

PROMOTE SAFETY

We want To promote responsible and safe driving habits by teaching members proper racing techniques and track safety.

PROMOTE KARTING

We want to promote karting to members and students because it isn't the most popular sport out there.
